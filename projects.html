✦ IN-DEPTH CASE STUDY SPOTLIGHT

Deep-Dive: Food Express Redesign

How we solved checkout abandonment, streamlined ordering velocity, and engineered a high-converting mobile experience for over 1.2M monthly active diners.

01

Research & Bottleneck Discovery

We tracked 1,200 user ordering sessions and identified that 42% of abandoned carts occurred during custom dietary allergen selections and unexpected tip additions.

  • • Conducted 24 in-depth customer interviews
  • • Mapped 5 competitor benchmark user flows
  • • Isolated checkout micro-friction points
02

UX Architecture & Motion Prototyping

Re-architected the checkout flow into an intuitive swipeable bottom sheet with 1-tap Apple Pay and instant live driver tracking animations.

  • • Modular expandable bottom-sheet UI
  • • Smooth turn-by-turn SVG driver route animation
  • • Zero-latency dietary allergen chips
03

Business Outcomes & Impact

Following the nationwide rollout, checkout completion increased significantly while customer support tickets regarding delivery status dropped by 64%.
